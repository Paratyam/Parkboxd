Local website has too many features, townspeople demand tour

By Jimmy Valmer, reporter and very handsome man

SOUTH PARK. Residents say Parkboxd, the site for logging and rating South Park episodes, now does so much that some users have only found half of it. This reporter went to investigate.

"I've seen the whole thing," said Jimmy, interviewing himself. "It's a t-t-terrific website. I mean, c-c-come on."

Your episodes

Log what you watch, give it up to five stars, like it and review it. Your diary keeps the dates, and your watchlist holds what's next. Build lists, rank the seasons in a tier list, and check your stats. Mr. Garrison will even grade your seasons on a report card.

Finding something to watch

Stan's Picks suggests episodes you haven't logged. The Chef's menu (the chef hat) serves a random one to match your mood. The search box also understands a description of what happened, for those who can't remember the title.

"The one with the f-f-fish sticks," said one expert. "That one's mine."

The characters

  • Butters' Book Club: one episode a week for everyone, with reviews and a vote on the next pick.
  • Cartman's Cage Match: two episodes enter, you pick the winner, and your true favourite gets the belt.
  • Kenny's Daily Mystery: guess the episode from a blurry picture. The hints are muffled.

Butters, Cartman, Kyle, Kenny and Stan also have their own profiles. Kyle posts a review of the day.

Streaks and badges

Log something every day to grow your Tegridy streak. Playing the Mystery or judging ten Cage Match fights also counts, and rain days cover the odd miss. Badges unlock new profile pictures and other perks.

Friends

Follow people to see what they're watching, like and comment on their reviews, and compare your taste match. If you follow each other, you can send messages. Found an episode someone needs to see? Tell them "You have to watch this." An invite link brings in new people.

Extras

  • Jimmy's roast: ask this reporter to roast any profile, using that person's real stats.
  • PC Principal: let him fix the wording of a review, or hear what he has to say to the characters about theirs.
  • Picture cards: share your reviews, Top 4, badges and more as pictures.
  • Rankings: see which episodes rank highest and which divide people most.
  • Hot takes: find the ratings furthest from everyone else's, and the people with the spiciest opinions.
  • Sound effects: available for the brave. Switch them on at the bottom of any page.

Rumours of secret modes could not be confirmed.

"Parkboxd is the b-b-best thing to happen to this town since me," Jimmy added. "Thank you, you've been a great audience."
